# AutoStonks Platform Features

## Agent Workstation
The Agent Workstation is the command center for automated trading. Here, you can deploy "Agents"—independent trading processes based on specialized strategy templates.

### Strategy Templates
1. **VWAP Mean Reversion**
   - **Concept**: Bets that the price will return to the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) if it deviates too far.
   - **Best for**: Stable, trending stocks during the regular session.

2. **Gamma Flip**
   - **Concept**: Exploits options market mechanics (Delta/Gamma hedging) to predict volatility shifts.
   - **Best for**: Major indices and high-liquidity ticker like SPY, QQQ, NVDA.

3. **Jarrod VWAP (Advanced)**
   - **Concept**: A proprietary variation of VWAP anchoring used for identifying institutional entry points.
   - **Best for**: Breakout trading and momentum following.

4. **Distribution Sniper**
   - **Concept**: Identifies "Price Distribution" patterns and snipes entries during liquidity gaps.
   - **Best for**: High-volatility tickers and early morning market opens.

## Real-Time Intelligence
- **Kronos Intelligence**: Provides AI-powered scoring for trade confidence.
- **Diagnostic Header**: Monitors system health (Alpaca, Intelligence, Options Chain) and real-time buying power.
- **Trade Log**: A searchable history of all AI decisions, math edges, and executions.
